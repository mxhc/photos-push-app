Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-08

  • mxhc photos push is used only by its owner, with his own Google accounts.
  • It requests only the photoslibrary.appendonly permission (plus openid email to confirm which account signed in). It can add photos to Google Photos. It cannot read, change, or delete photos that are already in the library.
  • The access tokens are stored only on the owner's private home server. They are not shared with anyone.
  • The app sends no data to third parties. It has no analytics and no advertising.
  • To revoke access, remove the app at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Contact: through the GitHub profile of the owner.
